# Manuscript notation in paper figures

The twenty active figures use `paper_notation.py` as their presentation vocabulary. It was checked against `revised.pdf`, SHA-256 `fd447e263b921e94203558e6b03e2a9aabaa03ab8deb5cec7545427aed4f112f`, Appendix A, Equations 2, 7–9, 12–15, and Appendix D.5 Equations 66–69. The plot recipe is `four-stage-manuscript-notation-11`; the zero-vector companions are retired, and the remaining appendix slots are consecutive. The selected μ and all retained measurement definitions are unchanged. No matching LaTeX source is present, so no source labels or theorem statements are changed.

The displayed $\boldsymbol{\mu}$ is the selected theory centre. By default, it is the full-vector average of 10,000 unconditional posterior clean references evaluated at the smallest SNR of the prespecified analytical extension on independent Gaussian inputs, from a separate Monte Carlo bank. The saved receipt identifies its finite-law identity, native initial SNR, separate estimation SNR, analytical grid index, extension depth, random stream and sample count, vector hash and Monte Carlo standard error. Finite-selected-SNR bias means this reference estimate is not asserted to be the exact training-data mean. The default source is `reference-min-snr`. The explicit `reference-initial` option retains estimation at the first native prediction noise level; `cached-targets` selects the exact weighted atom mean instead.

The default estimation SNR is the minimum of the fixed analytical grid, `SNR_T * 10**(-reference_snr_decades)`. It has analytical grid index zero and no native timestep or denoising-step index. It approaches the exact bank mean as SNR vanishes but remains a finite-SNR Monte Carlo estimate; its measured offset is not forced to zero.

The finite reference posterior, masses, radius and learned-reference error keep the declared atom law. Its exact weighted atom mean is stored separately from selected μ. The horizontal guide in A2 is the bank-mean-to-selected-μ distance divided by $\sqrt d$. It remains plotted and recorded in the caption/audit metadata without a “Reference limit” legend entry; the two means are not assumed to agree.

All vectors use bold symbols: $\mathbf{x}$, $\mathbf{z}$, $\mathbf{X}$, $\boldsymbol{\mu}$ and $\boldsymbol{\Delta}$. Scalars $e_t$, $p_t$, $L_t$, $V_t$, $R$, $g$, $\kappa_t$ and $\mathrm{SNR}_t$ remain unbolded. Axes use single-line division, never stacked fractions. Mathematical labels omit the implementation's `K` suffix; captions still identify the fixed empirical evaluation law and do not equate it with the model's complete training marginal.

The horizontal $T-t$ is exactly the existing chronological prediction index: zero means initialization, and the final prediction has $t=1$. It is not the native scheduler's training-noise label, and values are not reordered. In the Gaussian reference sweep, omitting the input would imply a generated state under the manuscript's convention; the explicit $\mathbf z$ avoids that mistake.

A10 and A11–A14 use the same complete evaluation seed cohort at every prediction within each prompt–target curve. Curve color is mean terminal SSCD over exactly those seeds, on the fixed [0,1] scale. Caption metadata identifies missing or invalid cohorts; it does not hide a changing seed denominator.

A14 `reference_branch_gap_per_prompt` shows $\|\bar{\mathbf{x}}_t(c)-\bar{\mathbf{x}}_t(\varnothing)\|/\sqrt{d}$ against $T-t$. The analytical reference difference is conditional minus unconditional at the same actual saved trajectory state and noise level. Under the existing single-target conditional assumption, $\bar{\mathbf{x}}_t(c)=\mathbf{x}^{\star}$, so its norm equals the saved opposite-sign distance $\|\bar{\mathbf{x}}_t(\varnothing)-\mathbf{x}^{\star}\|$. It therefore averages `direct_reference_target_error_rmse` once across the same complete fixed seed cohort, with a fixed color from those seeds' mean terminal SSCD. The learned branch gap, differences of error norms, and the radius-tail upper bound are different quantities and are not used. This adds no posterior or network evaluation; exclusions remain in `audit_data/reference_branch_gap_per_prompt_cohort.csv`.

A15 `corollary3_guidance_scale_vs_loss` plots `sqrt(L_T(c)/(d*SNR_T))` against the signed least-squares guidance coefficient for Corollary 3, Equation 54. The compact `x` remains the genuine forward-target loss mean `L_T(c)/(d*SNR_T)`; only its display takes one square root, after averaging the loss over draws. It is not the mean of per-draw roots. For each prompt–target pair, the fit minimizes `sum_seed ||(xhat_T(c;g)-mu)-a*(x_star-mu)||^2` over one scalar `a`, using the full fixed saved initial seed cohort at the actual first-prediction states. Gaussian-bank match status is recorded separately and is not assumed for this fit. Its shared target direction makes the joint coefficient equal to the arithmetic mean of the signed per-seed coefficients. Color is mean terminal SSCD over exactly those seeds; the labeled horizontal guide shows the actual configured `g`. The fit uses the selected reference mean, retains negative coefficients, and excludes incomplete cohorts or unidentifiable directions with saved reasons. It is a projection of the full guided clean estimate around μ, not a projection of `g*Delta_T`, and it does not estimate or change the configured generation guidance.

A16 `reference_variation_per_prompt` shows $V_t/\sqrt{d}$ against $T-t$, using the saved Equation-15 cross-step reference-variation norm-integral estimates `direct_prop5_variation_rmse` from `matched_updates`. The integrand is defined relative to the current unconditional posterior reference $\bar{\mathbf{x}}_t(\varnothing)$, not the selected global μ. Each prompt–target curve averages the already normalized estimates over the same complete fixed seed cohort at every positive-noise transition, with a fixed color from those seeds’ mean terminal SSCD. Its domain is $t=2,\ldots,T$, or $T-t=0,\ldots,T-2$; no final-prediction or output value is inserted, and a one-step schedule is not applicable. Finite nonnegative original estimates are retained when condition signs or the quadrature budget remain unresolved; refined interval midpoints are not substituted. A missing, invalid or structurally inapplicable required estimate excludes the entire curve with an explicit reason. `audit_data/reference_variation_per_prompt_cohort.csv` records cohort exclusions and numerical-status counts, while `audit_data/feedback_endpoints.csv` retains the source status, error estimates and bounds. These are numerical estimates, not certified exact integrals. The new compact reduction performs no integration, posterior evaluation or model inference.

Legend expressions identify the raw quantities; the shared distance-axis normalization is applied once. Initial recovery is a root **after** averaging squared errors, while trajectory plots retain their original summaries of individual normalized norms. Labels never perform additional normalization.

On fraction axes, $\Pr$ and CDF denote the saved weighted finite-sample fractions. Numerical unresolved mass remains in the denominators and is not claimed as observed success or statistical confidence. The words “unresolved” and “analytical reference only” remain where removing them would conceal numerical or measurement scope. Terminal bound expressions in axes and legends are abbreviated: every plotted bound still includes its saved independent finite-step correction before division by $\sqrt d$. The captions explicitly give the complete corrected quantities and deterministic, pathwise or probabilistic status. In `terminal_bound_coverage`, “Finite-step extension” is omitted from the artwork and retained in caption/audit metadata; no change to the original theorem applicability is implied. In scatter reference legends, $x=y$ denotes equality of the two plotted scalar coordinates, not latent-vector notation.

Colorbars read `SSCD`. For the initial recovery plot, captions explicitly retain the pair mean terminal-SSCD interpretation. Notation changes leave populations, weights, classifications and cached numerical tables unchanged. The requested per-prompt figures A10 and A11–A14 add compact tables reduced from existing trajectory scalars; older bundles missing these tables need explicit analysis-only reduction. Bundles already containing the guidance-fit table and current measurement-source receipts adopt this square-root display with `--plot`. Only bundles missing the fit or requiring a source-receipt update need analysis first; plotting never recomputes measurements. Rendering and tests were not executed during this source-only change.


Zero-companion figures are retired; saved measurements remain intact. New runs reject `--no-mu` and `--center zero`; historical saved configurations remain readable. The selected theory mean still defaults to `reference-min-snr`. See the [first-update command](figure_curation.md#author-commands--not-executed-here) for when an older bundle requires a compact-input refresh before plotting.
