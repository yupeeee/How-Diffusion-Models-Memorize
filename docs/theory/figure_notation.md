# Manuscript notation in paper figures

The active contract `projected-gap-error-1` uses the signed error $\mathcal{E}_t=\mathbf{u}_t^\top(\boldsymbol{\Delta}_t-\bar{\boldsymbol{\Delta}}_t)$, where $\mathbf{u}_t=\boldsymbol{\Delta}_t/\|\boldsymbol{\Delta}_t\|$ is computed from the actual learned gap. It may be negative and is never replaced by a norm, absolute value or clipped scalar. The margin is $\|\boldsymbol{\Delta}_t\|-\mathcal{E}_t-\mathcal{V}_t$. The variation remains $\mathcal{V}_t=[\int_0^1 \mathbf{u}_t^\top(\bar{\mathbf{x}}_{t-1}(\mathbf{x}^{\mathrm{cf}}_{t-1}+sg\kappa_t\boldsymbol{\Delta}_t,\varnothing)-\bar{\mathbf{x}}_t(\mathbf{x}_t,\varnothing))\,ds]_+$ for $t=2,\ldots,T$, with positive part after the signed integral. Exactly zero gap uses the explicit computational extension $\mathbf{u}_t=\mathbf{0}$, $\mathcal{E}_t=0$ and $\mathcal{V}_t=0$, without claiming a unit direction. Older bundles without this scientific contract require `bash run_all.sh --recompute-experiments`; historical gap-error norms cannot be relabeled by `--plot`. The six-figure placement and opacity update alone uses `--plot` on compatible current bundles. Compatible independent model probes and protected generation/SSCD/proximity data remain reusable.

The six selected figures use `paper_notation.py` as their presentation vocabulary. It was checked against `revised.pdf`, SHA-256 `fd447e263b921e94203558e6b03e2a9aabaa03ab8deb5cec7545427aed4f112f`, Appendix A, Equations 2, 7–9, 12–15, and Appendix D.5 Equations 66–69. The plot recipe is `four-stage-manuscript-notation-17`; publication uses six M1–M6 entries directly under `figures/`, with no appendix or per-step exports. Selected μ is unchanged; the condition and synchronization/terminal expressions use the explicit signed projected-gap-error refinement. No matching LaTeX source is present, so no source labels or theorem statements are changed.

The displayed $\boldsymbol{\mu}$ is the selected theory centre. By default, it is the full-vector average of 10,000 unconditional posterior clean references evaluated at the smallest SNR of the prespecified analytical extension on independent Gaussian inputs, from a separate Monte Carlo bank. The saved receipt identifies its finite-law identity, native initial SNR, separate estimation SNR, analytical grid index, extension depth, random stream and sample count, vector hash and Monte Carlo standard error. Finite-selected-SNR bias means this reference estimate is not asserted to be the exact training-data mean. The default source is `reference-min-snr`. The explicit `reference-initial` option retains estimation at the first native prediction noise level; `cached-targets` selects the exact weighted atom mean instead.

The default estimation SNR is the minimum of the fixed analytical grid, `SNR_T * 10**(-reference_snr_decades)`. It has analytical grid index zero and no native timestep or denoising-step index. It approaches the exact bank mean as SNR vanishes but remains a finite-SNR Monte Carlo estimate; its measured offset is not forced to zero.

The finite reference posterior, masses, radius and learned-reference error use one declared atom law. Its default is the empirical distribution of compatible complete cached source records: each record has equal mass and an exact duplicate atom receives the sum of its records' masses. This allows unequal atom priors without changing the manuscript notation for probabilities or posterior means. It does not identify the full checkpoint training distribution or verify the conditional single-target assumption. Its exact weighted atom mean is stored separately from selected μ. The horizontal guide in `unconditional_reference_convergence` is the bank-mean-to-selected-μ distance divided by $\sqrt d$. It remains plotted and recorded in the caption/audit metadata without a “Reference limit” legend entry; the two means are not assumed to agree.

All vectors use bold symbols: $\mathbf{x}$, $\mathbf{z}$, $\mathbf{X}$, $\boldsymbol{\mu}$ and $\boldsymbol{\Delta}$. Scalars $e_t$, $p_t$, $\mathcal{L}_t$, $\mathcal{V}_t$, $R$, $g$, $\kappa_t$ and $\mathrm{SNR}_t$ remain unbolded. Axes use single-line division, never stacked fractions. Mathematical labels omit the implementation's `K` suffix; captions still identify the fixed empirical evaluation law and do not equate it with the model's complete training marginal.

Axis and colorbar labels use 18-point text; tick labels remain 15 points and legends remain 12 points. The “Analytical reference only” annotation uses 10 points; other retained summary annotations remain 12 points. This typography update does not alter measurements or normalization.

The horizontal $T-t$ is exactly the existing chronological prediction index: zero means initialization, and the final prediction has $t=1$. It is not the native scheduler's training-noise label, and values are not reordered. In the Gaussian reference sweep, omitting the input would imply a generated state under the manuscript's convention; the explicit $\mathbf z$ avoids that mistake.

A10 and A11–A14 use the same complete evaluation seed cohort at every prediction within each prompt–target curve. Curve color is mean terminal SSCD over exactly those seeds, on the fixed [0,1] scale. Caption metadata identifies missing or invalid cohorts; it does not hide a changing seed denominator.

A14 `reference_branch_gap_per_prompt` shows $\|\bar{\boldsymbol{\Delta}}_t\|/\sqrt{d}$ against $T-t$. The analytical reference difference is conditional minus unconditional at the same actual saved trajectory state and noise level. Under the existing single-target conditional assumption, $\bar{\mathbf{x}}_t(c)=\mathbf{x}^{\star}$, so its norm equals the saved opposite-sign distance $\|\bar{\mathbf{x}}_t(\varnothing)-\mathbf{x}^{\star}\|$. It therefore averages `direct_reference_target_error_rmse` once across the same complete fixed seed cohort, with a fixed color from those seeds' mean terminal SSCD. The learned branch gap, differences of error norms, and the radius-tail upper bound are different quantities and are not used. This adds no posterior or network evaluation; exclusions remain in `audit_data/reference_branch_gap_per_prompt_cohort.csv`.

A15 `corollary3_guidance_scale_vs_loss` plots `sqrt(L_T(c)/(d*SNR_T))` against the signed least-squares guidance coefficient for Corollary 3, Equation 54. The compact `x` remains the genuine forward-target loss mean `L_T(c)/(d*SNR_T)`; only its display takes one square root, after averaging the loss over draws. It is not the mean of per-draw roots. For each prompt–target pair, the fit minimizes `sum_seed ||(xhat_T(c;g)-mu)-a*(x_star-mu)||^2` over one scalar `a`, using the full fixed saved initial seed cohort at the actual first-prediction states. Gaussian-bank match status is recorded separately and is not assumed for this fit. Its shared target direction makes the joint coefficient equal to the arithmetic mean of the signed per-seed coefficients. Color is mean terminal SSCD over exactly those seeds; a one-entry legend just below the horizontal guide near the right edge shows the actual configured `g` with a matching dashed handle. The fit uses the selected reference mean, retains negative coefficients, and excludes incomplete cohorts or unidentifiable directions with saved reasons. It is a projection of the full guided clean estimate around μ, not a projection of `g*Delta_T`, and it does not estimate or change the configured generation guidance.

The retained `reference_variation_per_prompt` table keeps its signed-integral/positive-part definition and original cohort receipts. It is not part of the six exported figures; the full measurement definition remains in the quantity dictionary.

Legend expressions identify the raw quantities; the shared distance-axis normalization is applied once. Initial recovery is a root **after** averaging squared errors, while trajectory plots retain their original summaries of individual normalized norms. Labels never perform additional normalization.

On fraction axes, $\Pr$ and CDF denote the saved weighted finite-sample fractions. Numerical unresolved mass remains in the denominators and is not claimed as observed success or statistical confidence. The words “unresolved” and “analytical reference only” remain where removing them would conceal numerical or measurement scope. Terminal bound expressions in axes and legends are abbreviated: every plotted bound still includes its saved independent finite-step correction before division by $\sqrt d$. The captions explicitly give the complete corrected quantities and deterministic, pathwise or probabilistic status. In `terminal_bound_coverage`, “Finite-step extension” is omitted from the artwork and retained in caption/audit metadata; no change to the original theorem applicability is implied. In scatter reference legends, $x=y$ denotes equality of the two plotted scalar coordinates, not latent-vector notation.

Colorbars read `SSCD`. Selected sparse-scatter opacity is 0.8; the pooled margin figure uses a uniform 0.1. Label and opacity changes never renormalize values or select observations. Compatible current scalar bundles use `--plot` for this six-figure curation; old scientific contracts still require explicit analysis. Rendering and tests were not executed during this source-only change.


Zero-companion figures are retired; saved measurements remain intact. New runs reject `--no-mu` and `--center zero`; historical saved configurations remain readable.
